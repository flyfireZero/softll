\section{Summary of Contributions}
We investigated whether continuous soft sets and nilpotent lattice operators can serve as an effective alternative to softmax attention in language models. Rather than connecting every token pair indiscriminately, our model embeds tokens into the unit cube $[0, 1]^D$ and models interaction as multi-attribute subsethood, using a nilpotent threshold to prune unrelated tokens to exact zero ($R_{ij} \equiv 0.0000$).

Our experimental findings across WikiText-2 and TinyStories demonstrate four core capabilities. Across 6.14 million tokens of autoregressive training, the architecture maintained an average of \textbf{55.06\% exact-zero attention weights} while generating fluent text and increasing lexical variety (+4.19\% Distinct-3). Under distractor stress tests with 16 noise facts, it pruned 66.9\% of distractor connections, cutting attention leakage by \textbf{49.4 percentage points}. In structured reasoning tasks, this subsethood inductive bias doubled Modus Ponens deduction accuracy (40.0\% vs. 20.0\%) and reduced bidirectional MLM loss (6.2041 vs. 6.2422). Furthermore, our causal KV-cache demonstrated $O(T)$ generation throughput at 180.6 tokens per second, matching uncached inference within single-precision floating point tolerance ($\Delta = 5.06 \times 10^{-7}$).

The primary trade-off is cross-entropy optimization: because the nilpotent threshold drops connections sharply to zero, the loss surface is steeper than softmax's smooth curve, resulting in higher validation loss (3.7455 vs. 3.4866 on TinyStories).

\section{Practical Implications and Future Work}
These results suggest two promising engineering directions. First, developing a dedicated fused CUDA kernel (FlashLattice) to evaluate the nilpotent threshold directly in GPU shared memory would bypass memory writes for zeroed entries, converting this 55\% theoretical sparsity into an estimated 1.5$\times$ to 2$\times$ wall-clock speedup. Second, combining lattice routers in lower layers for prompt denoising with standard softmax in upper layers for probability generation offers a practical hybrid path for foundation models.
